Findings & fixes

    🦇 Generator

    Randomness comes from crypto.getRandomValues with rejection sampling — no biased modulo shortcuts.

    How it works

    • Entropy is estimated from charset size × length, then reduced for predictable patterns: sequences, keyboard walks, repeats, years/dates, and common passwords (including leetspeak variants like p@ssw0rd).
    • Crack time assumes an offline fast-hash attack at 10 billion guesses/second — a deliberately harsh, attacker-friendly assumption.
    • Privacy: passwords never leave the browser, nothing is stored, and there are no network calls, accounts, or trackers.
